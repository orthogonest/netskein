# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals.
Equal participation privileges are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## 2. Encouraged Behaviors

While recognizing differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our community's expectations for positive behavior.
We also understand that our words and actions may be interpreted differently from what we intended based on culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward one another and act in a way that centers on our shared values, including:

1. Respecting **our community's purpose**, our activities, and the way we gather.
2. Interacting **kindly and honestly** with others.
3. Respecting **different viewpoints and experiences**.
4. **Taking responsibility** for our own actions and contributions.
5. Giving and accepting **constructive feedback** gracefully.
6. Committing to **repairing harm or negative impacts** when they occur.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and support **the well-being of our community**.

## 3. Restricted Behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors within our community. Actions, threats, and the promotion of these behaviors constitute a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Harassment:** Violating explicitly stated boundaries or engaging in unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks:** Making insulting, demeaning, or harassing comments directed at community members or a group of people.
3. **Stereotyping or Discrimination:** Characterizing someone's personality or behavior based on identity or immutable traits.
4. **Sexualization:** Behaving in a manner that is generally considered inappropriately intimate within the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Violating confidentiality:** Sharing or acting upon someone's personal information without their permission.
6. **Endangerment:** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm against any person or group.
7. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

### Other Restrictions

1. **Misleading Identity:** Impersonating another person for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions.
2. **Failure to Attribute Sources:** Failing to properly credit the source of the content you contribute.
3. **Promotional Material:** Sharing marketing or other commercial content in a way that falls outside community norms.
4. **Irresponsible Communication:** Failing to present content responsibly, including linking to or depicting other prohibited behaviors.

## 4. Reporting an Issue

Tensions can occur between community members even when they try their best to collaborate.
Not every conflict is a code of conduct violation.
However, when a violation incident occurs, it is important to report it immediately.

To report a possible violation, please create a confidential or flagged thread in our community **Discussions** space or reach out directly to the repository maintainers.

### Conflict of Interest Clause

If the reported incident involves one of the administrators, leaders, or the moderator team responsible for enforcement, the member in question will be **immediately and fully recused** from the review, investigation, and decision-making process to ensure objectivity.

### Investigation and Privacy

Community Moderators will investigate all reports of code of conduct violations by reviewing messages, logs, recordings, or interviewing witnesses.
Moderators will keep investigative and enforcement actions as transparent as possible while prioritizing the safety and confidentiality of the reporter.

## 5. Addressing and Repairing Harm

If an investigation finds that this Code of Conduct has been violated, the following enforcement ladder will be used to determine the best way to repair the adverse impacts:

1. **Warning**
   - **Violation:** A violation involving a single incident or a series of minor incidents.
   - **Consequence:** A private written warning from the Community Moderators.
   - **Repair:** A private written apology, acknowledgment of responsibility, and clarification of future behavioral expectations.

2. **Temporarily Limited Activities**
   - **Violation:** A repeated violation or a first incident of a more serious violation.
   - **Consequence:** A private written warning accompanied by a limited cooldown period on specific communication channels or interactions with specific community members.
   - **Repair:** Using the cooldown time to reflect on the impact of actions and exercising caution when returning to the community space.

3. **Temporary Suspension**
   - **Violation:** A pattern of repeated violations that failed to be resolved with warnings, or a single severe violation.
   - **Consequence:** A temporary suspension from all community spaces, tools, and communication channels for a specified duration, along with conditions for return.
   - **Repair:** Respecting the suspension period, fulfilling the specified conditions for return, and reintegrating properly.

4. **Permanent Ban**
   - **Violation:** A pattern of repeated violations that failed to change through other steps, or a violation so severe that there is no other way to maintain community safety.
   - **Consequence:** Access to all community spaces, tools, and communication channels is permanently removed.
   - **Repair:** There is no possible repair for this level of violation.

## 6. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and also applies when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing the community include using an official email address, posting via official social media accounts, or acting as an appointed representative at online or offline events.
